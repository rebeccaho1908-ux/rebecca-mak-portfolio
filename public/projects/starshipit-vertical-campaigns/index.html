Campaign design project

Starshipit Vertical Campaigns: eBook, social media, paid adverts

Summary

I developed a scalable creative campaign system for Starshipit’s industry verticals, designed to promote downloadable ebooks across food and beverage, beauty and cosmetics, and fashion and accessories. The work spanned landing page visuals, social tiles, and motion assets, with each campaign tailored to its audience while staying consistent within the Starshipit brand.

The creative approach combined clean layouts, soft gradients, bold typography, and bold imagery to make the content feel modern, credible, and conversion-focused. The result was a flexible campaign framework that could be adapted across multiple industries while maintaining a strong and recognisable visual identity.

Programs used:
InDesign Illustrator Photoshop Premiere Pro
