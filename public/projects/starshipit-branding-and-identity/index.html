Brand identity project

Starshipit Branding & Identity

Summary

I joined Starshipit during a key rebrand and helped translate the new identity into a broader visual system across web, sales, marketing, and brand touchpoints.

Along the way, I created a new brand guidelines document, new product illustrations for the website and presentations, gradient icon sets, sales decks, blog cover art, and email templates.

I joined Starshipit during their company rebrand, when the business was sharpening its position as an award-winning shipping fulfilment tech company focused on delivering great shipping experiences.

My role as the sole Digital Designer was to bring the new identity into Starshipit’s day-to-day brand system, including website content design, expo flyers, mailers, case studies, office wall decals, and trade-show display work.
