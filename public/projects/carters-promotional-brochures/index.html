CARTERS Promotional Brochures

Summary

I created promotional brochure campaigns for CARTERS that needed to work consistently across print, web, social, and in-branch touchpoints.

The work included brochure design plus the wider supporting collateral needed to roll each campaign out clearly and cohesively.

CARTERS Building Supplies has bi-monthly marketing campaigns which need to reach their customers on many platforms. Each promotion has a key giveaway item that becomes the focus of the campaign.

Whilst working at CARTERS I was in charge of creating the promotional brochures, fence banners, coreflutes, product ticketing, web banners, and social media tiles for each promotion.

Twice a year there are special ‘Power Up’ power tool promotions and trade breakfast events held at the CARTERS branches, so I also created event header boards, posters, and DL flyers for in-store promotions.
