Campaign case study project

Hello Molly x Starshipit Case Study Campaign

Summary

A multi-channel customer case study campaign for Starshipit, featuring fashion retailer Hello Molly and logistics partner SEKO. The goal was to turn a customer success story into a strong marketing asset that could support brand awareness, partner marketing, and sales conversations.

The final campaign included a hero case study video, paid social advert cutdowns, animated social assets, partner-ready materials, and a live case study webpage. It was distributed across Starshipit, Hello Molly, and SEKO Global LinkedIn channels.

Programs used:
Monday.com Premiere Pro

Role

I managed the campaign from planning through to launch, with responsibility across creative development, production coordination, post-production, and campaign delivery.

My role began with research and pre-production, where I prepared interview questions, developed briefing notes, helped shape the case study narrative, and wrote the main video script along with paid social advert variations. I also created and updated the storyboard, prepared meeting outlines for review sessions, and managed feedback and revisions with internal stakeholders and partners to keep the project aligned creatively and strategically.

On the production side, I coordinated filming logistics, booked and briefed contributors, prepared on-the-day shoot guidelines, and worked with videographer Josh Neilson from Media Album to capture the story effectively. This included planning locations, B-roll requirements, dashboard visuals, photography notes, and sequencing content based on shoot needs.

In post-production, I managed review rounds, implemented feedback, refined messaging, and supported final asset delivery. I also edited the social media advert cutdowns, created animated assets for social distribution, updated branding elements such as intros, outros, logos, and titles, and helped ensure all final assets were polished and ready for rollout.

Alongside the creative work, I managed the workflow in Monday.com, tracked tasks and due dates, coordinated approvals, supported partner sign-off, and helped deliver the final campaign across web, social, and partner channels.

Outcome

This project turned a customer story into a polished campaign that could be used across multiple marketing and sales touchpoints, including social media, website content, partner marketing, and sales enablement.

It demonstrated my ability to lead end-to-end campaign delivery, manage multiple stakeholders, and translate operational detail into clear, engaging content. It also highlighted my hands-on creative skills across content production, social advert editing, and animation. More broadly, it showed how a single customer story can be developed into a wider campaign with long-term value.
